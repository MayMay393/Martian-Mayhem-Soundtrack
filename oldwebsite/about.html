What is Martian Mayhem?

TL;DR: Basically, think of it as like, Minecraft, but 2D. And it's on Mars and is objectively worse.


Backstory

Martian Mayhem isn't really meant to be a game to make money - While it's definitive version will of course cost a couple of dollars, the main goal of Martian Mayhem was just to fulfill my childhood dream of making my own "Minecraft".

Anyway, the game itself is like one huge combination of all my old game ideas. Not ALL of them, I have more amazing ones for release in the future, but many elements of the game are taken from old projects of mine (But this is my first "Real" project.)

While this may be my first real game, it isn't close to the last. You'll see so much more from me throughout the coming years, and one day you'll see me take over the world. Or maybe not! Heh, heh.


What is the LORE about?

There's a lot of lore behind Martian Mayhem, but it's kept a bit obscure - I want people to be able to pretend the lore is theirs to create throughout their buildings and practices. But anyways...

Dott is a member of the ESXP, the Expanded Space Exploration Program. He joined the program in order to find use with his life after questioning how to face problems. On a mission to explore beyond the Solar System, he was interrupted around the 7-month mark when his steering wheel got jammed and started drifting into the orbit of Mars. He eventually crash landed, his shuttle left unpreparable, and faced with a new task: Make a new shuttle!

As an expert in survival, he's able to craft almost anything out of the tools on Mars. But he's missing an engine, and making one may prove to be very hard...

What is the Game Like?

Martian Mayhem is just a sandbox game in all it's glory. You'll be able to do a lot more as the game gets updated, so stay tuned. I want people to be as creative as possible, so the number of building materials to craft are very diverse. Expect to be able to fill your whole world with creations!

There's 3 save files right now, which is kind of small. More will come soon as the game expands, as well as world generation choices.

So yeah, Martian Mayhem is pretty much a sandbox on Mars. But don't underestimate it! It'll have more and more content throughout 2022.
